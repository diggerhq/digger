Drift Detection + Remediation continuously checks for differences between deployed infrastructure and your IaC source of truth.

When drift is found, OpenTaco helps teams close the loop with notifications and remediation workflows.

## What you get

- Scheduled drift detection across projects
- Notification channels such as Slack and GitHub Issues
- Remediation paths tied to existing PR/apply workflows

## Typical workflow

1. Enable drift detection at the organization level.
2. Enable drift on the projects you want monitored.
3. Set schedule and notifications (for example, Slack).
4. Review drift signals and remediate through your existing workflow.

## Why teams use it

- Surface out-of-band infrastructure changes quickly.
- Keep IaC and deployed infrastructure in sync over time.
- Reuse your current PR/apply operating model for remediation.
